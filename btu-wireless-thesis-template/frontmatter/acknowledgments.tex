The Acknowledgments section is optional, but it offers a meaningful, personal space at the culmination of your degree. Rather than copying standard boilerplate sentences, \textbf{take time to genuinely reflect on what and who was truly important for you} throughout this intensive research journey:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt,topsep=2pt]
    \item \textbf{Academic Mentorship:} Acknowledge your primary supervisor (\PrimarySupervisor) and secondary supervisor (\SecondarySupervisor). What specific intellectual freedom, critical feedback, or methodological guidance helped shape your scientific inquiry?
    \item \textbf{Lab Community \& Research Peers:} Did research associates, PhD candidates, or fellow students at the \ChairName\ help you configure hardware sensor testbeds, debug wireless transceivers, brainstorm architectures, or review draft sections?
    \item \textbf{External \& Industry Partners:} If your thesis was conducted in collaboration with an external partner (e.g., IHP -- Leibniz-Institut f\"ur innovative Mikroelektronik, an industrial enterprise, or a research cluster), express your appreciation for their data, equipment, or mentorship.
    \item \textbf{Personal Anchors \& Family Support:} Completing a thesis involves demanding phases, late-night debugging marathons, and self-doubt. Reflect on the parents, family members, partners, or close friends who offered moral encouragement, patience, and emotional balance.
    \item \textbf{Tone \& Style:} Write with genuine sincerity, humility, and academic dignity. Avoid excessive superlatives or overly informal slang, keeping the length between one-half and one page.
\end{itemize}
