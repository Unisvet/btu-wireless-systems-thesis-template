# BTU Cottbus–Senftenberg: Chair of Wireless Systems (Fachgebiet Drahtlose Systeme)
## Master's Thesis LaTeX Repository & Starter Kit

Welcome to the official repository for Master's and Bachelor's theses at the **Chair of Wireless Systems (Fachgebiet Drahtlose Systeme)**, Faculty 1 – MINT / Institute for Computer Science, Brandenburg University of Technology Cottbus–Senftenberg (BTU).

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

The modern, official LaTeX template is located in the [`btu-wireless-thesis-template/`](./btu-wireless-thesis-template/) directory:

- 📘 **[Template Quickstart & Manual](./btu-wireless-thesis-template/README.md)**: Instructions for compiling locally, in Overleaf, and configuring document metadata.
- 🎓 **[Structuring & Research Guidelines](./btu-wireless-thesis-template/GUIDELINES_FOR_STUDENTS.md)**: Chair recommendations on thesis architecture, formulating research questions, scientific methodology, experimental benchmarks, and defense preparation.
- 🤖 **[Mandatory AI Affidavit](./btu-wireless-thesis-template/frontmatter/declaration.tex)**: Compliant with official chair policy on the use of Generative AI tools (available both inside the thesis and as a [standalone 1-page PDF](./btu-wireless-thesis-template/declaration_standalone.tex) for Day-1 topic assignment).
